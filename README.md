# New Era CAD downloads

**Newest version: 1.0.245** - [Download NewEraCAD.zip](https://github.com/MAJIN32Bit/DR-STONE-downloads/releases/latest/download/NewEraCAD.zip), extract it, and double-click New Era CAD.exe.

What's new: Sidebar: drag its edge to make it wider (bigger photos) or thinner, and drag the dotted grip at its top to move it to the left or right (also View > Sidebar on the left). Notes: a drop-down under the tools for job notes, saved in the job; a red ! shows when there's a note.

Older versions and their notes: [Releases](https://github.com/MAJIN32Bit/DR-STONE-downloads/releases).

New Era CAD also checks here by itself: **Help > Check for updates**.
